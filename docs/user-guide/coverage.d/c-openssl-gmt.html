C callgraph contract coverage of OpenSSL libcrypto includes the SM4, Camellia, ARIA and SEED cipher constructors (EVP_sm4_cbc, EVP_camellia_256_cbc, EVP_aria_128_ccm, EVP_seed_cbc and their other modes), and OpenSSL libssl coverage includes the cipher list and TLS 1.3 ciphersuite setters SSL_CTX_set_cipher_list, SSL_set_cipher_list, SSL_CTX_set_ciphersuites and SSL_set_ciphersuites, whose list argument is reported as the configured cipher suites.
