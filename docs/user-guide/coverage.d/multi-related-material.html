A salt, nonce or iv drawn from a random source in one function and used in another is now reported. When the scan builds a call graph (--export-callgraph, --export-graph-fragment or --scan-dependencies), the value a random draw produces is followed into the functions it is passed to, up to three calls deep, and when it reaches a salt, nonce or iv argument of a known API the scan adds a related-crypto-material asset with that materialType at the draw line, with the draw's rule. Covered arguments: Python cryptography PBKDF2HMAC, HKDF and Scrypt salts, the CBC, CFB, CFB8, OFB and GCM ivs, the CTR nonce and the AESGCM and ChaCha20Poly1305 nonces; PyCryptodome AES.new iv= and nonce= and the PBKDF2, HKDF and scrypt salts; Go cipher mode ivs, the AEAD Seal and Open nonce, and the pbkdf2, hkdf, scrypt and argon2 salts; Java IvParameterSpec, GCMParameterSpec, PBEKeySpec and PBEParameterSpec; Rust OpenSSL symm ivs and pbkdf2_hmac salt, the pbkdf2 crate salt, Hkdf::new salt and the aes-gcm nonce; and Node createCipheriv and createDecipheriv ivs and the pbkdf2, scrypt and hkdf salts. A value that may be reassigned or overwritten before use, or that passes through a field, a collection or an interface call, is not reported.
