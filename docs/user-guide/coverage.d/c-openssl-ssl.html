C callgraph contract coverage includes OpenSSL libssl 1.1.1 and later: the TLS context and connection, the key-exchange group lists set through SSL_CTX_set1_groups_list, SSL_set1_groups_list and their NID-array and curves spellings, the signature-algorithm lists, the handshake, and the negotiated group, signature scheme and ephemeral key read back after it. The configured group list is reported from the call site, so an OpenSSL 3.5 hybrid group such as X25519MLKEM768, SecP256r1MLKEM768 or SecP384r1MLKEM1024, or a pure MLKEM768 group, is evidence on the context that offers it.
