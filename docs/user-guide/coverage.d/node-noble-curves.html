JavaScript and TypeScript callgraph contract coverage includes @noble/curves 0.9 to 2.x: ECDSA over secp256k1, the NIST P-curves and brainpool (signing, verification, key generation, public-key derivation and recovery, shared secrets), BIP-340 Schnorr, EdDSA over ed25519 and ed448, X25519 and X448, BLS12-381 signatures and aggregation, and the BLS12-381 and BN254 pairings. On 0.x and 1.x, imported without the .js suffix, the signature that sign returns carries its encoders and recoverPublicKey; 2.x sign returns bytes.
