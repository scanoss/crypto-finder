- A Go call through a struct field, `r.cache.Get()` or `s.discovery.get()`, now links to the method it calls. Before, the call was recorded with no callee type, so a method reached only that way had no callers and its finding read `no_callers`. The field's declared type comes from the struct declaration, resolved through the file's imports, in this or another package. A concrete field links to the method with an `exact` edge; a field of interface type links to the interface method and to every implementer, as `interface_dispatch` edges. The receiver must be the method's own receiver or a typed local or parameter, and the field a named, non-generic field of a struct the graph declares once. A longer chain (`r.a.b.M()`), a promoted embedded field, a func-typed or generic field, an indexed element, and a field two declarations of the same struct (build-tag variants) type differently stay unresolved as before.
