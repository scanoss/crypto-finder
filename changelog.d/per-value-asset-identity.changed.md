- The `finding_id` and `occurrence_key` of a per-value asset (one a parameter condition specialized to a resolved value) now include that value. Before, two values of one rule at one call, for example `SHA-256` and `SHA-512` at one `MessageDigest.getInstance(alg)`, shared both, so a consumer keyed on either merged them and could not address one value's reachability. Ids of per-value assets therefore change on upgrade. Ids of every other finding are unchanged.
