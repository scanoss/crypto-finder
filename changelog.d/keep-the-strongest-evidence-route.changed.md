- The chain budget is spent on the strongest evidence first: routes whose every call resolved statically, then routes that need a dispatch edge, then routes that need a `name_only` edge, and only within each of these one route per root first. Before, every kept chain of a finding could cross a `name_only` edge while a route free of one existed, so the chains contradicted the `reachable` verdict. A chain step now names the call site of its strongest recorded resolution, and call-site variants list the most certain site first, so the kept route's frames read the evidence it was chosen by. The stitched export also keeps direct routes before dispatch routes.
