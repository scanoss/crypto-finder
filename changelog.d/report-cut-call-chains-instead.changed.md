- The live callgraph export no longer caps chain depth (it was 32 frames) and no longer skips every chain of a finding with more than 100,000 routes, in the live and stitched exports alike. Such a finding now gets its sample of chains and reads `reachable` rather than `unknown`. The route walk skips branches that lead to no root, so its cost follows the chains built, not the routes the graph holds.
