- A finding now binds only to functions of the file it names, in the artifact it was found in: the scanned project, or the dependency its `dependency_info` names. Before, a finding matched any call-graph file whose path ended with the finding's path, and the tightest such function won. A finding in `util/seal.go` of one dependency could bind to `util/seal.go` of another dependency or to the project's `vendored/util/seal.go`, and in the callgraph and graph-fragment exports `symmetric.go` also matched `asymmetric.go`. The wrong function then gave the finding its `occurrence_key`, `call_chains`, reachability and `function_key`, and could specialize the finding by that function's arguments. On `step-ca`, 146 of 1,385 findings with a call chain were bound to another file, 133 of them in another artifact; on `powerauth-crypto`, 3 BouncyCastle findings were bound to the `META-INF/versions/15` copy of their file. A finding of a dependency the scan has no source directory for binds to nothing and reads `unresolved_reason: "no_containing_function"`. A rule-backed crypto entry point is now suppressed only by a finding in the declaring method's own file. `annotate` keeps its fallback for finding paths an imported fragment does not name exactly, but it now matches whole path segments.
- Occurrence keys and both exports no longer spend time proportional to the number of dependencies for each call-graph file. On `step-ca` with 155 dependencies, the `occurrence_keys` phase takes 20 ms instead of 0.8 s and `export` 1.3 s instead of 6.8 s.
