- `analysis.route_evidence` on live and stitched finding graphs (`graphfrag.ExportFindingAnalysis.RouteEvidence`): the strength of the finding's strongest route, rated by its weakest call. `direct` means every call resolved statically; `dispatch` means the best route needs an `interface_dispatch` (or `python_subclass_dispatch`) edge and no `name_only` edge, and the finding stays `reachable`; `name_only` means every route needs a `name_only` edge, the `unresolved_dispatch` case. A stitched finding proven only by a dependency's entry-point index carries none.
- `analysis.no_callers_only` on live finding graphs (`graphfrag.ExportFindingAnalysis.NoCallersOnly`): every root of the routes that support the verdict (those free of `name_only` edges, or all when none is) has `root_kind` `no_callers`, so the crypto is reached only from application code with no known callers. All routes count, not only the kept chains. Omitted when false.
